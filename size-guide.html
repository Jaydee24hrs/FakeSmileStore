Our cuts are relaxed, true-to-size streetwear fits. Measurements below are for the garment laid flat (±1.5 cm), plus the body measurement each size is designed to fit.

How to measure

  • Chest: around the fullest part of your chest, tape level under the arms.
  • Waist: around your natural waist, where joggers or shorts would sit.
  • Best trick: lay a hoodie or tee you already love flat and compare its chest width and length with the garment columns below.

Between sizes? Size up for the roomier, dropped-shoulder look; stay true-to-size for a cleaner fit. Not sure? Send your height, weight and chest on WhatsApp and we’ll recommend a size.

Headwear

Bucket hats and curved-brim caps are one size, fitting head circumferences of 56 to 60 cm (22 to 23.6″). Bucket hats have an adjustable inner band; caps have an adjustable rear strap. To measure, wrap a tape around your head just above the ears and eyebrows.
